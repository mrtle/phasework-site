# Vaporware Broadcast-System Redesign

## Direction
Rebuild the portfolio as a cinematic broadcast control surface inspired by the references: black technical framing, warm signal-red and amber fields, CRT-like scan texture, oversized condensed information, and disciplined modular grids. Preserve Vaporware’s exact V logo and use Satoshi throughout.

## What will change
- Replace the pale framed composition with a dark, full-width industrial broadcast interface.
- Create a compact masthead with the V mark, operational labels, coordinates, and clear Films / Studio / Contact navigation.
- Turn the opening into a bold signal panel with an oversized Vaporware title, live-status language, and a prominent featured YouTube film.
- Present the remaining YouTube projects as varied red, amber, and off-white transmission modules rather than a uniform card grid.
- Rework the studio and contact areas into technical specification bands with strong hierarchy and a clear email action.
- Add restrained scanlines, registration marks, segmented indicators, and load-in motion while keeping every film playable and legible.

## Content and behavior preserved
- Editing the existing `projects` list and pasting a normal YouTube link remains the only setup needed.
- Keep all five current projects, metadata, navigation targets, exact uploaded logo, and mobile support.
- Use the supplied images only as visual references; they will not appear in the website.

## Validation
- Check desktop and mobile layouts for overflow, collisions, and readable controls.
- Confirm all five YouTube embeds render and the page has no build or runtime errors.
