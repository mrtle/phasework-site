# Rebrand Vaporware with Field Standard

## Direction
Apply the uploaded Field Standard system to the existing Vaporware film portfolio in **Industrial mode** with the **Dynamism palette**: warm paper, near-black ink, and one high-visibility orange signal color. Keep the exact Vaporware V mark and Satoshi as the sole brand typeface.

## What will change
- Replace the current broadcast-heavy masthead and red hero with a calmer paper-first technical-documentation layout.
- Build the opening screen as a ruled equipment plate: logo and studio name, short factual positioning, selected-work status, oversized `VAPORWARE` word treatment, one signal cell, registration marks, and a compact readout strip.
- Present the featured video and remaining projects as precise, square-cornered film specimen plates with 1px rules, truthful project metadata, clear numbering, and strong scale contrast.
- Rewrite the visible copy to be short, concrete, and studio-specific: film direction, motion, VFX, sound, and color for technology companies. Remove invented coordinates, broadcast cosplay, and unsupported operating claims.
- Rebuild the studio and contact areas as an ink proof plate and a signal contact band, using only useful labels and project-start information.
- Use restrained plate-build and hover transitions with a reduced-motion fallback.

## Preserved behavior
- The exact uploaded V logo remains unchanged.
- Satoshi remains the only typeface.
- Editing the `projects` list remains the only setup required; standard, short, Shorts, and embed YouTube links continue to work.
- All five existing films, navigation targets, contact link, and social links remain available.
- Desktop and mobile layouts will be checked for overflow, readability, and working video embeds.

## Technical details
- Update the home page markup and global semantic design tokens/styles only.
- Follow the kit's three-color rule, visible 1px grid, square cells, restrained registration marks, bracket states, and readout strips.
- Avoid fake barcodes, fake certifications, invented coordinates, gradients, glow, shadows, rounded cards, and additional bright colors.
